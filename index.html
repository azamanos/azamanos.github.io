About

I work where deep learning meets structural biology, building methods that help scientists get from raw experimental data to molecular structure with less manual effort.

I am a PhD candidate at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, working with Archimedes at Athena Research Center. Most of my recent work is on cryo-electron microscopy: self-supervised models that pick particles out of noisy micrographs without hand-labelled data, and cryoPANDA, a dataset of 37 million particles from over 250 experiments. Before that I built HydraProt, a deep learning tool that predicts where water molecules sit around a protein. All of the code is open source.

Software & datasets

  • 2026
    Dataset

    cryoPANDA

    A benchmark dataset of over 37 million particles from 252 cryo-EM experiments, with notebooks for curation, linear probing and DINOv2 feature analysis.

    Code Data
  • 2025
    Method

    Cryo-EMMAE

    Self-supervised particle picking for cryo-EM. A masked autoencoder maps each micrograph to a representation space where clustering separates particles from noise, with no manual annotation.

    Code Data & checkpoints
  • 2025
    Analysis

    Amino acid propensities

    Helix, strand and loop propensities for each amino acid, computed from high-resolution PDB structures of soluble and membrane proteins using DSSP and MMseqs2.

    Code
  • 2024
    Tool

    HydraProt

    Fast, accurate prediction of explicit water positions in protein structures. A 3D U-Net samples candidate waters and an MLP scores them. Reads PDB and CIF files.

    Code Data & checkpoints
